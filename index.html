The journey

From the bean to the perfect cup.

Every cup we pour starts as a green seed on a mountainside far from here. Keep scrolling to follow one harvest from the farm to your table.

  2. 02Harvest
  3. 03Roast

  1. 01 / 05

    Origin

    Our beans grow on steep volcanic slopes, where cool nights slow the cherries down and give their sugars time to build.

    Altitude 1,600–2,100 m

  2. 02 / 05

    Harvest

    Pickers return to each tree several times and take only the ripe red cherries. The seeds are washed, dried on raised beds and sorted by hand.

    Hand-picked · 18–21 days drying

  3. 03 / 05

    Roast

    We roast in small batches twice a week and stop just after first crack, when caramel and cocoa notes are at their fullest.

    First crack 196 °C · Agtron 55

  4. 04 / 05

    Grind

    Roasted beans rest for a week, then meet the grinder seconds before brewing, so the aroma ends up in your cup instead of the air.

    Espresso grind · 250 µm

  5. 05 / 05

    Brew

    Water meets the coffee at 9 bar for 28 seconds. Eighteen grams in, thirty-six out, under a crown of hazelnut crema.

    9 bar · 93 °C · ratio 1:2

Our story

Every cup has
a story.

Velluto began in 2014 with a secondhand lever machine, four stools and one rule: never serve a cup we would not happily drink ourselves.

The name means velvet in Italian, which is how good crema should feel. We still chase that texture every morning, adjusting the grind as the weather changes and tasting each batch before it reaches the bar.

Today we roast our own beans, buy directly from the farms we visit, and still know most of our regulars by their order.

“We roast on Tuesday, taste on Wednesday, and argue about it until Friday.”The Velluto roasting team
  1. 2014

    A four-stool espresso bar opens with a single lever machine.

  2. 2017

    Our own 5 kg roaster arrives in the back room.

  3. 2021

    We begin buying directly from three family farms.

  4. 2026

    Twelve tonnes a year, still roasted in small batches.
